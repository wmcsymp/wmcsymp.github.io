About the Symposium

The Computer Science Department at William & Mary (W&M) is hosting its fourth Graduate Studies Symposium! The symposium will consist of faculty and graduate student panels, research talks, poster session, social events, and more!

Learn everything you wanted to know about getting a CS PhD or MS degree at W&M:

  • Funded PhD and MS/PhD degrees
  • MS in Computer Science, including AI/ML and Cybersecurity concentrations
  • MS pathways for non-CS Majors
  • Accelerated BS-to-MS pathway for W&M undergraduate students
  • Job Opportunities after an MS or PhD in CS
  • Cutting edge CS Research
  • Life as a Graduate student at W&M

Attend the symposium, in person or online, and you may qualify for an application fee waiver when you apply to our graduate programs. Waivers are subject to eligibility and available funding.

Venue and Parking

The W&M CS Symposium will take place at William & Mary, in the Integrated Sciences Center (ISC) - Room ISC 1127
